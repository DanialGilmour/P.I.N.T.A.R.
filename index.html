About P.I.N.T.A.R.

Predictive Intelligence for Network Tower Allocation & Risk-assessment

Developed by Team Bolehla for the ASEAN GeoAI Fusion 2026 Hackathon (HACK-MY-090).

P.I.N.T.A.R. is an enterprise GeoAI platform designed to optimize telecommunication tower allocation and climate risk mitigation across the entire Kuala Selangor district.

Study Area & Coverage

Kuala Selangor District, Selangor, Malaysia (incorporating Bestari Jaya, Tanjung Karang, Jeram, Ijok, and Kuala Selangor town centers).

Regional Datasets Integrated

  • WorldPop Population Grid (39,649 cells) — High-resolution demographic distribution (95,359 regional residents).
  • Existing Telco Network (67 Towers) — Base station towers & masts for coverage gap analysis.
  • Sentinel-1 SAR (2,755 points) — Active flood footprints mapping flood vulnerability.
  • SRTM DEM (30,584 points) — Terrain elevation & slope for line-of-sight signal propagation.
  • OpenStreetMap Vector Features — Building footprints, road networks & critical facilities.

Methodology

  • Risk Model — Flood proximity & slope stability scoring
  • Coverage Model — Spatial demand attraction & coverage gap analysis
  • Multi-Objective Ranking — Pareto-optimal tower placement
